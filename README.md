# Saltbound

An original third person sea adventure: sail a galleon across an unmapped sea, walk her decks, swim to shore, explore
islands on foot and gather a crew. Made by Daniel Ontiveros.

## Download

Open the [latest release](https://github.com/danxoco/saltbound-releases/releases/latest) and pick the file for your
computer:

* **Windows:** `Saltbound-VERSION-windows.zip`. Unzip it anywhere and double click `Saltbound.exe`. The first time,
  Windows may say it protected your PC because the game is not signed with a paid certificate: click More info, then
  Run anyway.
* **Mac (Apple silicon and Intel):** `Saltbound-VERSION-mac.zip`. Unzip it and drag `Saltbound.app` into Applications.
  The first time, right click (or Control click) the app and choose Open, then Open again, because it is not notarized
  by Apple yet. If it still refuses, open System Settings, Privacy and Security, and click Open Anyway next to Saltbound.
* **Linux:** `Saltbound-VERSION-linux.tar.gz`. Extract it and run `./Saltbound.x86_64` (needs Vulkan drivers).

Your voyage saves itself as you sail.

## Controls

| Action | Keys | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Walk, or steer at the wheel | W A S D or the arrow keys | Left stick |
| Set more or less sail at the wheel | W and S | Left stick up and down |
| Jump, then jump again in the air for Tomás's wind leap | Space | A |
| Gale Pearl leap, or a gust in the sails | Shift | RB or RT |
| Act: talk, take the wheel, open, board | E | X |
| Climb: walk, swim or jump into a ladder, net or vines; W and S climb, Space lets go | (no key) | |
| Drop or weigh the anchor | R | LB |
| Call the ship to you (hold it to go straight back aboard) | T | View |
| Switch to the crew member a badge asks for, or the next one | Q | Y |
| Read the letter in a bottle you just found | F | B |
| Raise the compass (once Abuelo Ciro gives it) | Hold C | L3 |
| Fish from the boat: cast, then hold to reel (let go while the line glows red) | E, hold E | X, hold X |
| Look around | Mouse | Right stick |
| Pause screen: chart, journal, satchel, codex, crew, controls, options (Q and E turn the pages) | Esc | Menu |

### At the guns

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Pick up a cannonball at a shot rack (E again puts it back) | E |
| Load an empty gun with the ball you carry, then man it | E |
| Aim while manning a gun | Mouse, or W A S D |
| Fire | Click, E or Space |
| Step away from the gun | Esc |
| Take a plank from the crate below, then hold E at a hole to patch it | E, hold E |
| Pick up a bucket, scoop the water below, throw it over the side at the rail | E |

### In a fight

| Action | Keys | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Strike (three in a row is a combo) | Left click or J | X |
| Heavy blow | Right click or K | B |
| Roll clear | Space | A |
| Block (raise it just as a blow lands to parry) | Hold Shift | Hold LB or LT |
| Special move of whoever you control | E | RB or RT |
| Next weapon you have won | Tab | Click the right stick |
| Hand over to the next of the crew | Q | Y |

The game speaks English and Spanish: choose in Options, Language.
