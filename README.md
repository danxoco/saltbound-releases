# Saltbound

An original third person sea adventure: sail a galleon across an unmapped sea, walk her decks, swim to shore, explore
islands on foot and gather a crew. Made by Daniel Ontiveros.

## Download

Open the [latest release](https://github.com/danxoco/saltbound-releases/releases/latest) and pick the file for your
computer:

* **Windows:** `Saltbound-VERSION-windows.zip`. Unzip it anywhere and double click `Saltbound.exe`. The first time,
  Windows may say it protected your PC because the game is not signed with a paid certificate: click More info, then
  Run anyway.
* **Mac (Apple silicon and Intel):** `Saltbound-VERSION-mac.zip`. Unzip it and drag `Saltbound.app` into Applications.
  The first time, right click (or Control click) the app and choose Open, then Open again, because it is not notarized
  by Apple yet. If it still refuses, open System Settings, Privacy and Security, and click Open Anyway next to Saltbound.
* **Linux:** `Saltbound-VERSION-linux.tar.gz`. Extract it and run `./Saltbound.x86_64` (needs Vulkan drivers).

Your voyage saves itself as you sail.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Walk, or steer at the wheel | W A S D or the arrow keys |
| Set more or less sail at the wheel | W and S |
| Jump, then jump again for Tomás's wind leap | Space |
| Gale Pearl leap, or a gust in the sails | Shift |
| Act: talk, take the wheel, open, climb | E |
| Drop or weigh the anchor | R |
| Call the ship to you (hold it to go straight back aboard) | T |
| Switch to another of the crew on foot | Q |
| Look around | Mouse (click to capture it, Esc to let go) |
